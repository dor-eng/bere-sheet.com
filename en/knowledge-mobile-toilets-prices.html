Mobile Toilet Rental for Events

Today, placing mobile toilets in places with no access to built toilets is more accessible and available than ever.

Mobile Toilet Rental: Types, Prices and Tips for Choosing Wisely

If you need such toilets, whether for an outdoor event, a construction site or camping, it is important to check not only the quality but also the prices at which they are offered. In the following article we will explain how to choose the model best suited to your specific needs and get the maximum return on your investment, so that you can enjoy maximum quality at minimum cost.

What Are Mobile Toilets and When Are They Essential?

Mobile toilets are a temporary, portable sanitation solution that can be used at a wide range of events and in many situations. Their main advantages include, among others, great flexibility in location, the ability to suit events of any size, and the option of providing sanitary comfort even in remote places or places with no permanent infrastructure.

Modern eco mobile toilets offer environmentally friendly solutions, with water-saving systems and biodegradable materials. In addition to the sanitary advantages, they allow you to hold events in places that were once logistically inaccessible. Today, their use has become standard, with constant improvement in quality and user experience.

Mobile toilet rental is essential in various situations, including:

  • Events: weddings and bar mitzvahs, business events, parties, sporting events and festivals.
  • Field work: construction sites, industrial plants and archaeological digs.
  • Leisure and trips: camping, youth camps and gatherings in the field, such as stargazing in the desert.
  • Conferences, fairs and markets: areas with a steady flow of visitors and no built toilets.

Price Differences Between Types of Chemical Toilets for Rent

The first factor affecting the price of mobile toilets is the specific model of facility you want to rent. These are the two categories we offer:

Chemical Toilet Stalls

The most common type of chemical toilet is a standard, basic and relatively cheaper unit. Such mobile toilets suit events where usability is the main requirement. These include, for example, summer camps of youth movements, trips with large groups or mass parties.

Beyond the standard model, we at Bereshit Mobile Toilets offer several additional models of mobile toilets for rent, at varying price levels:

  • Mini toilet stall: ideal for places with limited access or narrow areas, especially suited to high-rise building sites.
  • Accessible chemical toilets: an especially spacious and large stall, with a level entrance with no step and grab handles around its interior.
  • Luxury mobile toilets: an upgraded stall with a recirculating water flush system and a sink.
  • Air-conditioned solar VIP stall: a designed, well-equipped stall with an advanced water flush system, air conditioner, LED lighting and a built-in sink. Such toilets suit, for example, VIP areas at concerts, film sets, small events in the field and the like.

Luxury Toilet Trailers

These upscale models are rented at higher prices. They are especially suited to events with many participants, where you particularly want to invest in the guests' well-being, for example: a bar mitzvah or wedding, business and company events, and unique launch events held in nature, and more.

The trailers are well planned and equipped with air conditioning, lighting, music and luxury accessories. Here too you can find several different models to choose between according to parameters such as price, size and accessibility:

  • 2-stall luxury trailer: divided into a women's stall and a men's stall, suited to events of up to 200 guests.
  • 4-stall accessible luxury trailer: divided into 4 individual stalls with a dedicated entrance for people with disabilities with an advanced lift, suited to events of up to 350 guests.
  • 6-stall luxury trailer: divided into a women's wing and a men's wing with a spacious lobby in each wing, suited to events of up to 400 guests.

Good to know: when you rent toilet stalls from us for the long term, we provide a professional pump-out and ongoing maintenance service, to maintain maximum usability, comfort and hygiene.

Not sure which type of mobile toilets suits your event? Contact our professional, experienced team and we will be happy to give you personal advice and a no-obligation quote.

Other Factors Affecting the Final Cost

There is no single answer to the question "How much does it cost to rent mobile toilets?". Not only because of the specific model, but because many factors affect the price, including:

  • Number of stalls required: the number of stalls needed for the event directly affects the total price. The number of participants must be matched to the stalls required, and large quantities may earn you a discount.
  • Event duration: the rental period affects pricing. Usually, long-term rental is at a more favourable rate.
  • Event location: the geographic location of the site affects the cost of the toilets, because of transport costs. We provide full service throughout the country and at any site, and are experienced in placing mobile chemical toilets in all field conditions.
  • Maintenance and pump-out service: in the case of long-term rental, it is important to keep up ongoing maintenance and pump-out of the waste tank. Sometimes this service involves an additional cost.

Mobile Toilet Rental: Comparing Price Against Quality and Service

Although the prices of mobile toilets are an important aspect, it is important to remember that when it comes to sanitation, service and quality are no less important. A high price does not guarantee excellent service, and a more convenient price does not necessarily mean poor quality. So if you are comparing mobile toilet prices, conduct a thorough market survey and examine additional parameters to get the best quality at the most worthwhile price. Here are the factors worth checking in advance:

  • Range of models: the option to choose between different models, at different quality levels.
  • Safety and operations: make sure the supplier's operations teams put safety first, to protect the service providers, the workers and the people attending the event.
  • Sanitary treatment: mobile toilets must provide comfort, hygiene and high usability. Make sure the company supplies stalls that have been disinfected and checked, and provides cleaning and waste pump-out services at the required frequency.
  • Professionalism and availability: is the company available for every problem and question, and does it provide full, personal and attentive service.
  • Experience and reputation: before choosing a supplier, always check its previous experience, whether it is experienced in all types of events at different locations in Israel, and whether it is considered a reliable and professional supplier.

Bottom line, if you want to know how much it costs to rent mobile toilets, you need to consider all the logistical and sanitary variables. In addition, it is important to also take into account the quality of the product, the cleanliness, the safety and the professionalism of the company, so that you receive the best service. Our goal is for you to receive a comprehensive answer that matches your specific needs exactly.

Frequently asked questions

What is the main parameter affecting the price?

The most significant factor is the type of stalls. Naturally, standard models will be cheaper than luxury models.

Does a low price guarantee good service?

No. When it comes to sanitation, service is no less important than price. So make sure it is a combination of the two: full, professional service and an attractive price.

Do the service providers advise on matching the number of stalls?

Yes. It is recommended to use the company's professional team to match the number of stalls to the number of participants at the event or workers on site.

Is the maintenance service included in the price?

Not always. Before ordering the stalls, make sure the price includes hygiene and sanitary service, such as cleaning and disinfection, a thorough inspection before delivery, and pump-out at the desired frequency.
