Mobile Toilets for the Field: Toilet, Shower and Tap for Any Conditions

When we head out into nature or organise an event outside urban areas, we face a significant challenge:

Why Do You Need Field Toilets?

The lack of sanitary infrastructure. This is not just a matter of comfort; it is an issue of public health, protecting the environment, and respect for participants. A toilet in the field is not a luxury but a basic need that directly affects participants' experience and the overall success of the event or activity.

Using mobile toilets in the field ensures:

  • Maintaining participants' hygiene and health
  • Protecting the environment from pollution
  • Comfort and well-being for participants, especially at long events
  • Compliance with regulations and legal requirements for mass events

Types of Mobile Toilets for the Field

When it comes to sanitary solutions for the field, there is a wide range of options, each with advantages and disadvantages suited to different situations. Dry (composting) toilets, for example, offer an environmentally friendly solution suited to long-term use, but require more complex maintenance and may be less comfortable for users. A folding field toilet is a lightweight solution ideal for short trips or small groups, but is limited in capacity and in comfort for prolonged use. DIY solutions may suit small groups and short stays in the field, but usually do not meet the hygiene and comfort standards required for large events or extended stays. By contrast, mobile chemical toilets offer the optimal solution for large groups, long stays in the field and maximum ease of use, while maintaining high standards of hygiene and ease of maintenance.

Advantages of Chemical Toilets in the Field

As noted, every solution has its own advantages and disadvantages, but there is no doubt that mobile chemical toilets offer several significant advantages:

  • Mobility and flexibility: easy to move and place in any area.
  • Large capacity: suitable for use by hundreds of people before emptying is required.
  • High hygiene: contain chemicals that break down waste and prevent odours.
  • Easy to maintain: can be cleaned and serviced quickly.
  • Resistant to harsh weather conditions: suitable for all seasons.

Chemical toilets are designed especially for mass events, construction sites, festivals, and any field activity that requires a sanitary solution for large groups of people.

How Does It Work?

Chemical toilets work on a simple but effective principle. At the bottom of the toilet is a tank containing a special chemical solution. When the toilet is used, the waste falls into this tank. The chemical solution breaks down the waste, neutralises odours, and prevents bacteria from multiplying. This ensures a hygienic and pleasant environment to use, even when the toilets are in intensive use over time.

Features of a Quality Rental Service

When choosing a supplier of mobile toilets for the field, it is important to pay attention to several features:

  • A range of products and solutions: a quality supplier will offer a variety of mobile toilet types, including options for large events, construction sites, and private events.
  • Hygiene, sterility and maintenance: it is important to choose a company that is meticulous about cleaning and disinfecting the toilets, and provides ongoing maintenance services throughout the rental period.
  • Professional and comprehensive service: excellent customer service, including advice on choosing the right solution, professional installation, and support throughout the event.
  • Attractive price: while quality is critical, it is also important to find a supplier offering competitive, transparent prices with no hidden costs.
