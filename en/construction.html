Construction sites

Mobile toilets for construction sites

If you manage a construction project, your role includes providing a sanitation solution for the workers. Sites usually have no access to a permanent sewage system, so dedicated chemical toilets are needed, together with ongoing maintenance to keep them hygienic.

A self-contained, sealed unit: no water, no sewage

Chemical toilets operate as a self-contained unit that needs no connection to water or sewage infrastructure. At the base of the unit is a sealed holding tank with a dedicated chemical solution that breaks down organic waste, disinfects the tank and neutralizes odors.

The structure is designed for natural ventilation and made of durable materials that are easy to clean. This lets you place the units anywhere on the site, with no advance preparation.

For everyone working in the field, from laborers to supervision teams

  1. Active construction sites

    From residential buildings and office towers to urban renewal projects (TAMA 38) in the heart of dense areas.

  2. Paving and development works

    On roads and in open areas.

  3. Logistics and industrial sites

    Complexes under construction, factories being renovated and redeveloping industrial zones with no available sewage access.

How many units, and which model

It all starts with a consultation call to understand the needs of the site: the expected number of workers and the planned duration of the work. Based on that, we choose the model together, from a standard, durable toilet unit through upgraded models to accessible units that meet safety and accessibility regulations.

Standard chemical units serve as self-contained units and are well suited to construction sites. As a rule of thumb, for events or sites with up to 200 participants we recommend 2-3 toilet units, continuing at a similar ratio as the number of people grows.

Periodic emptying throughout the life of the project

The service does not end with placement. Maintenance includes thorough cleaning and disinfection of the unit, periodic emptying of the collection tank using dedicated pumps, replacement of the chemical solution that breaks down waste and neutralizes odors, and refreshing of fragrance supplies.

Hygienic emptying of the holding tank is especially important at active construction sites. Before delivery we carry out thorough functionality checks and disinfection.

Frequently asked questions about toilets for construction sites

Why are chemical toilets essential at a construction site?

They provide a hygienic, convenient sanitation facility where there is no access to a permanent sewer, ensure workers' welfare, and protect public health and on-site regulatory requirements.

Is a water or sewage connection required?

No. The units operate as a self-contained, sealed system and require no infrastructure, so they can be placed anywhere on the site.

Can I rent for short periods?

Yes. You can rent short-term for one-off jobs, or long-term for active construction sites, according to the schedule in the field. Long-term rental

How much does it cost to rent chemical toilets for a construction site?
