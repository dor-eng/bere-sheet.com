Luxury restroom trailers

Luxury restroom trailer rental

Standard mobile toilets are no longer the accepted standard, and event producers prefer to rent luxury mobile toilets. A luxury restroom trailer combines practicality with comfort and style, and suits weddings in nature, corporate events, music festivals and VIP areas.

The models: Shachar, Tamar and VIP

  • Shachar model (standard): a giant toilet-paper dispenser, a mirror on the door, a service shelf, an interior lock and a waste collection tank.
  • Tamar model (upgraded): recirculating flush water, a cover at the base of the toilet, a three-roll toilet-paper dispenser, a 250-litre waste collection tank and perimeter shade nets.
  • Solar air-conditioned VIP unit: air conditioning, a sink with running water, soap and fragrance dispensers.

Inside a luxury toilet trailer: a porcelain toilet, a designer sink, a large mirror and paper towels.

A permanent-building restroom experience, even in the field

The luxury restroom trailers include spacious interiors and premium fittings, air conditioning, LED lighting and sound systems. The stalls are air-conditioned and spacious, with electronic faucets, scented soaps and air fresheners.

The trailers offer users full privacy, with separate stalls for men and women, and are equipped with cleaning and disinfection systems that prevent unpleasant odors. They can be adapted in number of stalls and fittings, according to the needs of the event.

Choose by number of guests, event duration and the need for accessibility

  1. Air-conditioned solar VIP stall

    For those who prefer a single high-end stall. All fittings run independently on a solar system, except the air conditioner, which requires a 220V electricity connection.

  2. 2-stall trailer

    Relatively compact, usually divided into a women's stall and a men's stall. Suitable for events of up to 200 guests.

  3. 4-stall accessible trailer

    4 individual stalls and a dedicated entrance for people with disabilities with an advanced lift. Includes air conditioning, sound systems, interior and exterior LED lighting, electronic faucets and scented soaps. Suitable for events of up to 350 guests.

  4. 6-stall trailer

    The largest trailer. A women's wing and a men's wing, each with a spacious entrance lobby to prevent crowding. Suitable for events of up to 400 guests.

What you need on site to run a trailer

Luxury restroom trailers can be placed anywhere, without a connection to permanent sewage and water infrastructure. However, to run all the trailer's systems, such as the air conditioner and the electronics, a 220V single-phase electricity connection is required.

Luxury models usually also require a water hose and easy truck access, to operate the upgraded systems such as air conditioning and sinks with running water.

  1. Electricity

    220V single phase.

  2. Water

    Usually a water hose, to run sinks with running water.

  3. Access

    Easy truck access.

Clean throughout the event

Advanced chemical treatment systems neutralize odors and bacteria, alongside high-frequency cleaning and maintenance, so the restroom environment stays clean throughout the event. At the end of the event the waste is handled in a controlled manner, and the area is left clean.

We provide full service, from the moment of booking to the end of the event, including a dedicated attendant who makes sure the restrooms stay clean and pleasant throughout use. The 4-stall trailer can also be rented for long periods, including pumping and maintenance.

Frequently asked questions about luxury trailers

What is the difference between a standard stall and a luxury trailer?

The difference lies mainly in the level of hospitality and fittings. A standard stall is an efficient, basic solution that suits any terrain without special preparation. A luxury trailer offers air conditioning, background music, LED lighting, electronic faucets and separate women's and men's stalls.

Are special infrastructure connections needed?

No connection to permanent sewage and water infrastructure is needed. However, using the air conditioner and the electronic systems requires a 220V single-phase electricity connection.

Are luxury toilets accessible to people with disabilities?

Yes. The 4-stall trailer has a dedicated accessible stall equipped with an electric lift. The stall is wider than usual and includes grab rails and adapted maneuvering space.

Can a luxury trailer be placed anywhere in Israel?

Yes. Luxury toilets are available for rent for events in the north, center and south of the country, with no location limit.
