Accessible Toilets: Standard Dimensions and How to Build an Accessible Stall

An accessible toilet stall is a facility designed specifically to meet the needs of people with physical disabilities.

What is an accessible toilet stall?

Its main features include a larger turning space, supportive grab bars and adapted fittings. In Israel, accessibility regulations require the installation of accessible toilet stalls in public buildings and at mass events, as part of the effort to promote equality and social inclusion.

Portable toilets: how do they work?

Portable toilets, or chemical toilets, are a temporary, mobile solution for sanitary needs in places without permanent infrastructure. They consist of a plastic or metal stall, a collection tank and a ventilation system. Such toilets come in various sizes and models, from a single stall to multi-stall units. The stalls are transported to the required location by truck and are maintained on a regular basis by professional rental companies.

Accessible portable toilets: how do you make toilets accessible for people with disabilities?

To turn a portable toilet into an accessible stall, several adaptations are needed:

  • Larger stall dimensions: an accessible stall must be large enough to allow a wheelchair to move freely.
  • Ramp installation: the ramp allows easy access into the stall.
  • Grab bars: sturdy bars installed for support and safety.
  • Raised toilet seat: to ease the transfer from the wheelchair.
  • Wide door: to allow comfortable wheelchair entry.
  • Accessible fixtures: sink, mirror and toilet paper dispenser at a suitable height.

4 significant advantages of portable accessible toilets

An accessible toilet stall brings several significant advantages:

  • Access for everyone: enables people with disabilities to take full part in events and public activities.
  • Flexibility and convenience: the stalls can be placed anywhere, which increases convenience for users.
  • Regulatory compliance: using portable accessible toilets ensures compliance with the legal and regulatory accessibility requirements.
  • Improved image: providing accessible toilets shows a commitment to equality and inclusion, which improves the organisers' image.

Key points when choosing an accessible toilet stall

When choosing an accessible toilet stall, several factors should be considered:

  • Dimensions and turning space: the stall must be large enough (at least 150×150 cm) to allow a wheelchair to turn fully.
  • Aids and safety: sturdy grab bars, an emergency button and sufficient lighting are essential.
  • Ease of use: a toilet at a suitable height, an accessible sink and a mirror at the right angle increase comfort.
  • Durability and easy maintenance: durable, easy-to-clean materials ensure hygiene is maintained over time.
  • External access: a stable ramp and a sufficiently wide door are critical for comfortable access.

How do you choose a toilet rental service?

Choosing a toilet rental supplier, especially for an accessible stall, requires careful attention. Here are some points to look out for before ordering:

  • Wide range: a quality supplier will offer a selection of toilet stalls, including accessible toilets in various sizes and designs, suited to different types of events and locations.
  • Cleanliness and appearance: the stalls should be clean, well maintained and attractive, including the area around the stall.
  • Safe use of the facilities: make sure all facilities, especially in an accessible stall, meet safety standards and are maintained regularly to prevent faults.
  • Reliability and professional service: a professional company provides dependable service, including meeting schedules, regular maintenance and a quick response to problems.
  • Tailoring to the customer's needs: the ability to adapt the service to specific requirements, such as the exact placement of the stalls or the addition of special fixtures, is a significant added value.
