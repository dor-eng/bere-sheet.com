Pricing

Mobile toilet pricing

The question "How much does it cost to rent mobile toilets?" has no single answer. The price depends on the type of units, the quantity, the rental duration, the location and the maintenance. Here is a breakdown of what it consists of, so you arrive at the quotation prepared.

From hundreds of shekels to thousands of shekels

The price of chemical toilets for events varies according to the size of the event, its season, location and requirements, and can range from hundreds of ₪ to thousands of ₪. As the quantity grows, the price is affected accordingly.

These are general ranges, not a fixed price list. An exact price is set in a quotation based on your details.

  1. Standard toilet unit

    The most affordable, suited to large events and work sites. A cost of hundreds of shekels per unit.

  2. Luxury toilet unit

    With a sink, lighting and upgraded design, for weddings, bar mitzvahs and launch events. The cost may reach thousands of shekels per unit.

  3. Luxury restroom trailers

    Rented at higher prices, and especially suited to events with many participants.

Five factors that determine the final cost

  1. Type of units and level of fittings

    The most significant factor. Naturally, standard models are cheaper than luxury models.

  2. Number of units

    The number of units directly affects the total price, and should be matched to the number of participants. Large quantities may earn you a discount, making the price per unit more favorable.

  3. Rental duration

    The rental period affects pricing. Long-term rental usually comes at a more favorable rate.

  4. Location and site conditions

    Location matters because of delivery costs. Site accessibility, delivery distance and difficult road conditions may raise the cost.

  5. Maintenance and pumping

    Tank emptying, cleaning, disinfection and refreshing supplies affect the total price. This service sometimes carries an additional cost.

For a single event or a long period

The facilities can be rented short-term for one-off jobs and events, or long-term for active construction sites and ongoing projects, according to the schedule in the field.

For long-term rentals we provide a professional pumping and ongoing maintenance service, to keep the units usable, comfortable and hygienic.

Frequently asked questions about pricing

How much does it cost to rent chemical toilets for a construction site?

You can rent toilet units across a wide price range. The price depends on the model, the number of units, the rental period and the site location, and also includes the frequency of maintenance and pumping services throughout the period.

Is the maintenance service included in the price?

Not always. Before ordering, make sure the price includes hygienic, sanitary service: cleaning and disinfection, a thorough check before delivery and pumping at the desired frequency.

Does a low price guarantee good service?

No. When it comes to sanitation, the service matters no less than the price. Make sure you get a combination of full, professional service and an attractive price.

Will you help us match the number of units?

Yes. Our team will match the number of units to the number of participants at the event or workers on the site. For a first estimate you can use the unit calculator.
