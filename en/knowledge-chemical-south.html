Features of portable chemical toilets

Portable toilet stalls are designed to provide a sanitary, hygienic and safe solution in changing field conditions, from dense urban areas such as construction sites in Beer Sheva, through remote chemical plants in the Negev, to unique event locations in the Arava. A quality toilet stall includes several components, depending on the specific model you choose:

  • Durable exterior: the stall is made of rigid plastic (high-density polyethylene), resistant to weather, sun radiation and strong disinfectants.
  • Sealed holding tank: at the base of the stall is a large-capacity tank, designed to collect waste in a sterile and safe manner.
  • Basic sanitary fittings: a toilet with a comfortable seat and a dedicated toilet paper dispenser.
  • Supplementary hygiene system: advanced models have an internal handwashing sink installed, with soap and paper towel dispensers.
  • Locking mechanism and privacy: the stall door is fitted with an internal lock that includes an external "vacant/occupied" indicator to ensure privacy.
  • Ventilation openings: the stall includes built-in ventilation grilles at the top, which allow air circulation and prevent the build-up of odours and gases.
  • Lighting and comfort: luxury models include interior lighting, air conditioning, a carpet and even a lobby.

How do portable chemical toilets work?

Portable toilets operate as completely self-contained systems, with no need for a connection to running water pipes or a sewage system. At the heart of the operation is the sealed holding tank, into which a dedicated chemical solution is added in advance. This solution performs three actions at once: it breaks down the organic waste, disinfects the tank of bacteria, and neutralises unpleasant odours using active fragrance components.

Airflow in the stall is based on the principle of natural pressure; the ventilation openings at the top of the structure let hot air and gases escape and fresh air enter. When the holding tank reaches its capacity limit, it is pumped out by a dedicated service truck (a "septic tanker"), which draws out the contents in a sterile manner. After pumping, the tank is thoroughly rinsed and refilled with fresh chemical solution, allowing continuous, hygienic and safe use over time in any field conditions.

How do you choose the right portable toilets?

If you are looking for portable toilets in the south or any other part of Israel, it is important to consider a few points that will help you refine your choice and provide the best solution for your guests or employees:

  • Matching the number of participants: this is the most important measure for preventing crowding and queues. As a rule of thumb, for events or sites of up to 200 participants, we recommend 2-3 toilet stalls. At an event of about 500 guests, increase the number to at least 5 stalls, and continue at a similar ratio as the number of people grows.
  • Type of solution required: choose between standard chemical toilet stalls, which serve as stand-alone units and are very suitable for construction sites and simple field events, and luxury toilet trailers. The trailers include several stalls in a single unit, offer an upgraded user experience, and are especially suited to festive events that require a high aesthetic standard.
  • Accessibility: you must check whether accessible toilets are required for people with disabilities or an older population. These stalls feature an especially wide entrance door that allows smooth wheelchair entry, and a large interior with a handrail that allows independent, safe use without relying on outside help.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do chemical toilets work without a connection to sewage infrastructure?

The toilets operate as a self-contained system that includes a sealed holding tank with a dedicated chemical solution. The solution breaks down the organic waste, disinfects the tank and neutralises odours, while upper ventilation openings allow natural circulation of fresh air.

What are the main parameters for choosing the number and type of stalls?

The choice is based on the number of participants (a ratio of 1-2 stalls per 100 people); the nature of the activity (standard stalls for construction sites versus luxury trailers for festive events); and the need for accessibility for people with disabilities or an older population.

What does the regular maintenance service include?

The service includes a full operational package: cleaning by a dedicated staff member, real-time checks and hermetic pumping of the holding tank by a "septic tanker". Complementary solutions such as fragrance systems and background music can also be added.

Are the portable toilets resistant to the weather conditions of southern Israel?

Yes. The stalls are made of rigid plastic (high-density polyethylene) that resists strong sun radiation, extreme temperatures and the weather typical of the south and the desert.
