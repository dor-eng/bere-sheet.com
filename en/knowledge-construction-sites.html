Portable Toilets for Construction Sites: How Many Stalls You Need and How to Choose

If you manage a construction project, your role includes arranging a sanitary solution for your workers.

Chemical toilets for construction sites: a portable sanitary solution for workers

Most of the time, the site has no access to a permanent sewage system, which requires renting chemical toilets dedicated to construction sites. At Bereshit we offer you high-quality portable toilets that combine comfort, safety and usability, together with regular maintenance service to maintain maximum hygiene.

Chemical toilets for construction sites: how does it work?

Chemical toilets operate as a self-contained, closed unit that does not require a connection to water and sewage infrastructure. At the base of the stall is a sealed holding tank containing a dedicated chemical solution; this solution is responsible for breaking down the organic waste, disinfecting the tank and neutralising unpleasant odours over time. The structure itself is designed for efficient natural ventilation and made of durable materials that are easy to clean, and regular maintenance includes periodic pumping of the tank with dedicated pumps, and renewal of the chemicals and fragrances.

Who is the construction site toilet rental service for?

The need for accessible, quality sanitation solutions is shared by a wide range of projects in the real estate and infrastructure sector. Placing toilets on a construction site provides suitable sanitary conditions for the professionals in the field, from workers, through visitors, to supervision and management teams. This is a safety and regulatory necessity that ensures continuity of work throughout the day. Portable toilets are intended for construction sites such as:

  • Active construction sites, from building residential buildings and office towers to urban renewal projects (TAMA 38) in the heart of dense areas.
  • Paving and development works on roads and in open areas.
  • Logistics compounds under construction, factories under renovation and redeveloping industrial zones with no available access to a permanent sewage system.

  • Step 5 – Regular maintenance: the service does not end with placement; it includes a meticulous maintenance package covering pumping, cleaning, disinfection and renewal of chemicals on a regular basis, to maintain a high level of hygiene throughout the rental period.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why are chemical toilets an essential solution on a construction site?

They allow a hygienic and convenient sanitation facility to be set up in places with no access to a permanent sewage system. Placing them ensures workers' welfare and protects public health and regulatory requirements in the field.

Is a water or sewage connection required to place the stalls?

No. Portable chemical toilets operate as a self-contained, closed system and require no infrastructure at all, which lets us place them at any point on the site without advance preparation.

What does the regular servicing of toilets on site include?

Our maintenance includes a full hygiene package: thorough cleaning and disinfection of the stall, periodic emptying of the collection tank with dedicated pumps, and replacement of the chemical solution that breaks down waste and neutralises odours.

Can toilets also be rented for short periods?

Yes. Our service is flexible and adapted to project needs. The facilities can be rented short-term for one-off jobs or long-term for active construction sites, according to the schedule in the field.

How much does it cost to rent chemical toilets for a construction site?

You can rent toilet stalls across a wide price range, with the price determined by weighing the type of model, the number of units required, the length of the rental period and the location of your site. The cost also includes the frequency of the maintenance and pumping services required throughout the period.
