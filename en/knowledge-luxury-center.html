Luxury Toilets in the Center

If you are planning an event in central Israel, at a location without decent toilet stalls or any connection to water and sewage, it is worth ordering luxury toilets from our company.

Luxury Toilet Rental in the Center

These are well-designed, attractive trailers that offer guests the best comfort and space, blend into the venue as an integral part of it, and do not detract in the slightest from the sense of luxury and good taste.

Which Events Is It Suitable For?

First of all, luxury toilet rental in the Center can suit any type of event. For example, it can suit business events such as concept events, executive events, team-building days, special shows and parties, launches, fairs and conferences. Of course, the emphasis is on events held in outdoor areas, whether in nature or in areas not adapted for large crowds, where there is no adequate, dignified and satisfactory provision for toilets. In addition, luxury toilets in the Center suit family events, with an emphasis on relatively large events such as a bar or bat mitzvah or a wedding.

What Do Luxury Toilets Mean?

If you type the phrase "luxury toilets in the Center" into Google, you will find quite a few companies offering such solutions. Even so, it is important to understand that luxury toilet trailers differ from standard trailers in several respects. In addition, this is an exceptionally large toilet stall, in fact the largest in Israel, which greatly improves the experience of staying in the trailer. Beyond that, there is also an option to add a sink for hand washing, it has a service shelf for placing personal belongings, it is ventilated and has ventilation screens all around, and it has a fixed mirror on the door. It is also the only stall with a giant toilet paper dispenser that holds three rolls. And if that is not enough, the stall also has a recirculating flush system responsible for flushing the toilet after use. It is a system with a cover at the bottom of the toilet bowl that hides its contents, so the use is very similar to airplane toilets and is more pleasant.
