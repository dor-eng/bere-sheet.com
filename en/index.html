Before you order

Do I need a water or electricity connection?

Chemical toilets and handwashing stations need no connection at all. A VIP stall needs 220V for the air conditioner only. A luxury trailer needs 220V single phase. A shower needs water and electricity.

How many stalls does an event need?

It depends on the number of guests and the duration of the event. Our calculator gives an estimate in a minute, and we will be glad to refine it by phone.

What goes into the price?

The type of equipment, the rental period, the quantity and the project location. Details on the price page.

Are there accessible units?

Yes. An accessible chemical unit and a 4-stall trailer with an accessible entrance and a lift. Accessible toilets.

Can I rent for months?

Yes. All products are available for monthly rental, including pumping and ongoing maintenance. Long-term rental.
