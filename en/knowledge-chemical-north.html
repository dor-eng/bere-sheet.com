Good to Know

Chemical Toilets for Rent in the North

When we want to organise a large event or work site, there are dozens of logistical needs we must consider.

Chemical Toilets for Rent in the North

Of course, each of them differs in character and in the demands it places on us as organisers. For example, if we want to organise a wedding in nature, our needs will include luxury catering, suitable attire, flower arrangements and more. If, on the other hand, we are running a large youth movement camping trip, the logistics will look completely different: we will need to think about a kitchen, the safety of the children's activities, transporting trip equipment and so on. So in the same location, events can look entirely different. Even so, there is one need shared by all types of events and sites we may have to organise: the fact that all people need to use the toilet.

Portable toilets

We are used to taking our home toilet for granted. Most of the time, there is no real question about where we will relieve ourselves. This changes, of course, when we organise a large event in nature, or in places without a proper plumbing system in place. This is exactly why we need to order portable toilets in the north. Portable toilets are toilet stalls that can be moved from place to place efficiently and inexpensively, and they meet the most basic physical needs of participants. Even in places where no sewage system has been set up, we can provide proper, high-standard toilets.

When will we need portable toilets?

There are dozens of cases in which we would want to rent portable toilets. The first that comes to mind are events with many participants held in a natural environment, that is, in field conditions. This category includes large camping trips (by youth movements or the formal education system), celebrations held in nature such as weddings or bar mitzvahs, festivals attended by hundreds and thousands of people, and more.

In addition to events held in nature, portable toilets are also commonly used at various types of construction sites. Because the plumbing system at construction sites is also under renovation, or does not yet exist at all, there is a need for a place to relieve oneself. Portable toilets are the solution to this issue and provide an excellent temporary solution for workers and all visitors to the site.

Why in the north of Israel?

In recent years the north has experienced significant momentum across many fields such as construction, culture, employment and more. Anyone who comes to the north, and certainly anyone who lives there and spends most of their time there, will see that at any given moment dozens and hundreds of events are taking place, making use of the amazing nature of the Galilee and the Golan, as well as a building boom that scatters many construction sites across the region. Portable toilets for rent in the north therefore meet many needs in the area, both at construction sites and at the many events held in nature.

Types of portable toilets

Portable toilets are a category that includes several types of products, meeting the needs of every customer. Because every event and site is slightly different, the toilets also vary between them, providing a precise fit.

  • Chemical toilets: chemical toilets are portable toilets that use chemicals to break down waste and hide the unpleasant smell. Chemical toilets can be used in the north at all types of events and sites.
  • Portable toilets in a luxury trailer: if we are organising a wedding in nature, it is important to us to maintain a prestigious atmosphere, even when the event takes place in field conditions. To provide this atmosphere, you can rent portable toilets housed inside a luxury trailer. The trailer suits events where a prestigious atmosphere matters, such as a wedding, a launch or a bar mitzvah. The trailer is air-conditioned and designed to look like a proper toilet in a prestigious, permanent building.
  • Accessible toilets: accessibility is an important and just cause even when we leave the built-up city. At events with many participants, there is a good chance that participants who use wheelchairs will also attend, and they must of course be taken into account. The good news is that there are models of portable toilets that are fully accessible for people with disabilities, and they can be rented for any type of event, just like regular portable toilet stalls.

Professionalism comes first

It is unpleasant to say, but toilets and plumbing are complex businesses with no room for mistakes or amateurism, since the consequences can be, how shall we put it, smelly.

It is therefore very important to rely in this field only on the most professional and experienced service, which can provide us with the toilets at the highest standard. It is very important to understand that there is no room for amateurism in installing portable toilets and removing their waste.
