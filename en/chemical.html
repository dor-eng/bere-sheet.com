Chemical toilets

Chemical Toilet Rental for Events and Sites

Portable chemical toilets for weddings, festivals, fun days and outdoor events, nationwide. The units are self-contained and need no connection to water or sewage.

An outdoor event with a proper sanitary solution

Outdoor events, whether a wedding, a music festival, a family fun day or a team-building day, pose a challenge: how to give guests comfort, hygiene and privacy while protecting the natural environment.

Bereshit supplies portable chemical toilets for all types of events nationwide: toilets for business and private events, VIP toilets, showers and hand-wash stations.

The units are built to withstand all weather conditions. Renting chemical toilets for an event does not require a water connection or sewage infrastructure at the placement area.

Every level of chemical toilet, including VIP

Bereshit supplies every level of portable chemical toilet, including VIP portable toilets, for private and business events in open areas and out in nature. Different models can be rented for productions in nature, outdoors or anywhere without ready infrastructure, and the number of units can be matched to events of any size.

The service rests on high-quality, durable products and on experienced professional crews, with an emphasis on product quality, competitive prices, service and reliability.

Every day Bereshit handles thousands of units across the country, for clients such as regional authorities, the Ministry of Defence, municipalities, real-estate companies, national youth movements, production companies and private customers.

Which models suit your event

The choice depends on the type of event, the expected number of guests and the budget.

  • Standard model: the basic model, rented mainly for large mass events, summer camps, trips and construction sites.
  • Upgraded model: a wider, more impressive unit for stylish events.
  • Solar air-conditioned VIP unit: for events and VIP areas. For details see the Luxury trailers page.
  • Luxury trailer: for events where it matters to give guests comfort and a feeling of cleanliness.
  • Accessible model: an accessible, spacious and large unit with grab handles around the interior, level entry with no step and an interior lock. Long-term rentals include pumping and maintenance. See also Accessible toilets.

How chemical toilets work

Portable chemical toilets meet the basic need when a regular restroom cannot be used, for example in field conditions. They are not connected to a central sewage system. The toilet drains into a collection tank, and disinfectant, cleaning and fragrance agents are used in the process.

The disinfectant is a blue chemical liquid that keeps the unit clean. In most cases it contains a compound called formalin.

The contents of the collection tank are pumped out from time to time: for an event rental, right when the units are collected; for a longer rental, such as a construction site, periodically according to use, by connecting a hose to the toilet with a pump at its end.

The main concern when renting toilets for an event is unpleasant odours. Bereshit units are well maintained and delivered clean, shiny and smelling fresh.

Professional installation

Chemical toilets should be installed by experienced professionals. Professional installation ensures safe use of the disinfectants, correct pumping of the contents and use of suitable materials to prevent odours. If handled incorrectly, the chemicals can harm both whoever does the work and whoever uses the unit.

Customers order and place units wherever they choose, and receive them clean, in working order and ready for use. The units are exposed to rain and sun, so they must be durable and properly built.

The number of units in an order can be adjusted for events of any size, and showers and hand-wash stations can be added. See also hand-wash station rental.

How much does chemical toilet rental cost

The price varies by order duration, type of toilets, level of equipment, complexity of execution, scale of the event, season and location, and can range from hundreds to thousands of NIS (₪). As the quantity grows, the price is affected accordingly.
