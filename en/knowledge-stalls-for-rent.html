Toilet Stalls for Rent | Mobile Toilets and Ready-Made Units

Anyone who has ever organised a large event or worked at a remote construction site knows that one of the most basic challenges is providing a suitable answer to participants' sanitary needs.

Mobile Toilets: A Comprehensive Guide to Choosing Toilet Stalls for Rent

Mobile toilet stalls have become the most essential and widespread solution in Israel, and demand for them continues to grow from year to year. In this guide we will provide all the information you need to choose the solution best suited to you, and to ensure that your project or event takes place in comfortable, hygienic sanitary conditions.

Situations in Which Renting a Mobile Toilet Stall Is Recommended

  • Construction and infrastructure sites: where there is no sewage connection, but there is an obligation to provide workers with basic conditions throughout the working day.
  • Mass events and outdoor events: festivals, concerts, field weddings and sporting events require placing ready, available toilets for a large crowd.
  • Remote areas or open nature: organised trips, camping and tourist sites without permanent infrastructure.
  • Temporary work or short-term projects: sites under construction, temporary markets, fairs and exhibitions.
  • Emergencies and natural disasters: chemical toilets for rent are also needed in areas where infrastructure has been damaged and an immediate response is needed to maintain hygiene and the health of the public and workers.
  • Agricultural sites or open areas: field work over long hours without access to permanent toilets.
  • Renovations or work in existing buildings: when the permanent toilets are unavailable or unsuitable for use during the work.

How Does a Mobile Toilet Stall Work?

A mobile toilet unit is an easily transportable structure that operates as a self-contained, closed system. It does not require a connection to water and sewage infrastructure, so it can be placed almost anywhere. At the base of the stall is a sealed holding tank into which waste and liquids drain. Inside the tank is a dedicated chemical that breaks down the waste, reduces the formation of gases and unpleasant odours, and helps maintain a hygienic environment over time.

The toilet seat is located in the upper part of the stall, and sometimes also a flush mechanism allowing a basic rinse after use. In more advanced models you can also find a sink with clean water, a soap dispenser, a mirror and internal lighting, depending on the need and the level of fittings requested.

The stall structure itself is designed for effective ventilation, through dedicated openings that allow air flow and reduce odours, and is made of durable materials that are easy to clean. The door includes a simple locking mechanism with a vacant/occupied indicator, to allow comfortable and safe use even at events with many participants.

How Is the Price of Renting a Ready-Made Toilet Stall Determined?

Before you compare prices, it is important to understand that the price of mobile toilet rental is affected by several material variables. Without examining all the parameters, the comparison may be inaccurate and even misleading.

  • Stall type and level of fittings: a standard toilet stall is the cheapest and suits large events or work sites, at a cost of hundreds of shekels per stall. By contrast, if you rent luxury toilet stalls with a sink, lighting and upgraded design intended for events such as weddings, bar mitzvahs and launch events, the cost may reach thousands of shekels per stall or trailer.
  • Number of stalls ordered: naturally, the more stalls you order, the higher the total price. However, in some cases you may receive a quantity discount and the price per unit will be more worthwhile.
  • Length of the rental period: renting chemical toilets for one day is cheaper than renting for several days or an extended period, among other reasons because of the need for ongoing maintenance service.
  • Maintenance and service costs: maintenance service such as emptying tanks, cleaning, disinfection, replenishing chemicals and spraying fragrances usually affects the total price.
  • Location and field conditions: access to the site, transport distance and complex road conditions may raise the cost due to more complex logistics.

Different Types of Chemical Toilet Stalls for Rent

As a company specialising in the rental and maintenance of mobile toilets, we offer a wide range of solutions that you can choose from according to your specific needs:

Shachar Model: Standard Model

A spacious toilet stall with a basic design, containing a giant toilet paper dispenser, a mirror mounted on the door panel, a service shelf for placing personal belongings, an option for internal locking and a waste collection tank. These stalls are designed and manufactured to withstand all weather conditions and can be placed wherever required, with no prior preparation.

Air-Conditioned Solar VIP Stall: Premium Model

An advanced, high-level designed toilet stall that offers a comfortable, clean and aesthetic user experience. Mobile Toilet Trailers: Luxury Models

These trailers contain two, four or six stalls, divided for use by women and men. These are the most luxurious and exclusive models, and they include air conditioning, lighting and a hand-washing station with a sink and mirror. The trailers are especially suited to special events such as weddings, bar mitzvahs, birthdays or upscale business events.

Please note: unlike the standard models, luxury stalls require a 220v electrical connection on a separate phase, a water hose and truck access. As a result they are less suited to events taking place in the heart of nature, without a connection to water systems or convenient truck access.

Accessible Toilets

At most public events, especially those requiring a licence, you will be required to place an accessible toilet stall in accordance with the accessibility regulations. But regardless of the legal obligation, this is basic social and equal fairness. These toilet stalls are larger, include grab rails, a ramp or ground-level entrance, and a wide door allowing entry with a wheelchair.

We at Bereshit offer you two types of accessible stalls for people with disabilities: one from the standard stall series and one as part of renting a four-stall luxury toilet trailer. Beyond suiting people with disabilities, these models also suit the older population, so it is recommended to rent them for events intended for seniors.

Tips for Choosing a Mobile Toilet Supplier

Choosing the right chemical toilet supplier is not only a matter of price. To ensure an event or project that runs smoothly, it is important to pay attention to several key factors:

  • Company experience and reputation: choose a supplier with proven experience in the field and the ability to handle logistical and sanitary challenges. It is recommended to work with a company that supplies toilet stalls to well-known bodies or significant projects.
  • Service and maintenance operation: good service does not end with placement. Check whether the supplier offers ongoing maintenance, cleaning, replenishment of consumables and pump-outs throughout the event or rental period, and whether there is an available response in case of a fault.
  • Transparency and fair prices: it is important to understand exactly what the price includes: delivery, placement, removal, cleaning and maintenance. Make sure there are no hidden costs and that all the required services are included in the quote.
  • Range of solutions and fit to the need: a professional supplier will offer a range of standard and luxury models, including accessible solutions as needed.
  • Quality, durability and safety: the stalls must be stable, resistant to all weather conditions and maintained to a high standard. In addition, make sure the supplier works according to safety procedures and employs a professional, skilled team.

Frequently asked questions

Do mobile toilet stalls require a connection to water or sewage infrastructure?

Most models operate as a self-contained, closed system and do not require a connection to infrastructure. However, luxury models usually require a 220V electrical connection, a water hose and convenient truck access to operate the upgraded systems such as air conditioning and sinks with running water.

How are aesthetics and hygiene maintained during extended use?

Hygiene is maintained by using dedicated chemicals that break down waste and neutralise odours, alongside a structural design that allows effective ventilation. Upgraded models have a flush system and a toilet cover that hides the contents. In addition, it is recommended to make sure the supplier carries out disinfection and a thorough functional check before every delivery and provides ongoing maintenance services.

What are the most important parameters when choosing a mobile toilet supplier?

Beyond price, it is important to examine the company's experience and reputation, the scale and availability of its service and maintenance operation, the range of solutions offered, and the level of transparency regarding associated costs such as delivery and removal.
