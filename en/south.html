Mobile toilet rental in the South

From construction sites in Beersheba, through remote factories in the Negev, to event locations in the desert and the Arava. Toilet units suited to extreme weather and difficult access roads.

Construction, industry and events in the field

Southern Israel sometimes presents unique field conditions: extreme weather and remote areas with difficult access roads. The units are made of rigid plastic (high-density polyethylene), resistant to strong solar radiation, extreme temperatures and strong disinfectants.

The toilets operate as a self-contained system, with no connection to water pipes or sewage: a sealed holding tank with a dedicated chemical solution, and ventilation openings at the top of the unit.

A rule of thumb for the number of units

Up to 200 participants: we recommend 2-3 toilet units. For an event of about 500 guests: at least 5 units, continuing at a similar ratio as the number of people grows. Roughly, 1-2 units per 100 people.

Calculate how many units you need

  1. Standard units

    Self-contained units, well suited to construction sites and simple field events.

  2. Luxury trailers

    Several units in one trailer, for festive events such as a wedding in the desert.

  3. Accessible units

    A wide door for wheelchair entry, a large interior space and a rail for independent, safe use.

Ongoing maintenance at every location in the South

A dedicated staff member is responsible for ongoing cleaning and checking the units in real time. The holding tank is pumped hermetically using a dedicated service truck (a "septic tanker"), a service that is especially vital at long events and active construction sites.

Complementary options such as automatic fragrance dispensers and background music can be added.

Frequently asked questions about mobile toilets in the South

How do chemical toilets work without a connection to sewage infrastructure?

The toilets operate as a self-contained system with a sealed holding tank and a dedicated chemical solution. The solution breaks down organic waste, disinfects the tank and neutralizes odors, and upper ventilation openings allow natural air circulation.

What are the key parameters for choosing the number and type of units?

The number of participants, at a ratio of 1-2 units per 100 people; the nature of the activity, standard units for construction sites versus luxury trailers for festive events; and the need for accessibility for people with disabilities or an older population.

What does the ongoing maintenance service include?

Cleaning by a dedicated staff member, real-time checks of the units and hermetic pumping of the holding tank by septic tanker.

Are the mobile toilets resistant to the weather conditions of southern Israel?

Yes. The units are made of rigid plastic (high-density polyethylene), resistant to strong solar radiation, extreme temperatures and the weather hazards typical of the South and the desert.
