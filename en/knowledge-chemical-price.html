Chemical Toilet Prices: How Much Does Portable Toilet Rental Cost?

Chemical toilets for rent can be seen at many open-air events and at productions of all sizes.

On different types of toilets for events in open spaces

Sometimes only a single toilet unit is seen, and sometimes there are several units scattered across the venue for the use of guests, invitees or those taking part in the production itself. For those interested in renting chemical toilets, a fair price is a huge advantage when considering this option, as well as the different types and models of toilets. They can be rented for any period you want, and combined with additional solutions for a more comprehensive answer.

For any event you want

Today there are companies that offer portable chemical toilet rental services, which can be placed anywhere in the field. Whether at large or small concerts, at festivals held in different parts of the country, at a company event with quite a few guests, at some production, or even at a family party in nature to which all family members, close and distant, were invited. Chemical toilets suit field conditions and no special sewage infrastructure is needed to operate them. They contain a special disinfectant, responsible for keeping the unit clean and sterile, and they can serve camping trips and even weddings held in large open spaces.

Chemical toilets for rent: prices

Renting different models and types of chemical toilets carries a different price, which reflects a combination of several factors such as: the length of the order, the type of toilets, the level of equipment, the complexity of execution, and so on.

The price of chemical toilets for events varies according to the scale of the event, its season, its location and its requirements, and can range from hundreds of ₪ to thousands of ₪; and of course, as the quantity rises, the price is affected accordingly.

A perfectly tailored solution

These toilets mostly come in standard sizes and definitely stand out in the field. They are stylish and blend in well with the site, and are often located at a point further from the area where the celebration takes place. At the same time, they are accessible, and you can even find portable chemical toilets designed for people with disabilities. If desired, freestanding handwashing stations, which also require no special infrastructure on site, can be placed beside them. All of this can be rented for a pre-agreed period, giving an excellent solution available to all guests and an ideal answer, fully tailored to a wide variety of events.
