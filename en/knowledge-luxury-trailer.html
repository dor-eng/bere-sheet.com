Designer Luxury Toilets for Your Event: Bereshit's Luxury Toilet Trailer

Imagine you are producing a special event, such as a luxury wedding or a glittering music festival.

Luxury Toilet Trailer

Instead of standard, cramped mobile toilets, your guests find a luxury trailer, carefully designed and fitted to the highest standard.

A luxury toilet trailer is not just a functional solution but a unique and impressive experience. It is designed in an elegant style, with soft lighting, upscale furnishings and decorative details that are pleasing to the eye. Also, instead of a cramped toilet stall, there is a spacious, well-lit stall with a quality porcelain toilet, a designed wash basin, a large mirror and even soft paper towels.

So if you are planning a private or business upscale outdoor event, you need to arrange suitable infrastructure for it, including upmarket, comfortable, spacious toilets that are pleasant to use. Here at Bereshit you will get a complete answer to these needs with designed, large and spacious mobile toilets, planned especially for upscale events.

As noted, luxury mobile toilets are high-level mobile toilets that offer a range of services and upgrades not available in standard mobile toilets. This makes them ideal for special events, weddings and other events.

Is Ordering a Luxury Toilet Trailer Worth It?

You have surely invested a great deal of money in your event to get perfect hospitality in every detail, whether in the best food, in tables and comfortable lounge corners and more, so you will surely want to keep giving your guests pampering service when they go to relieve themselves too. Mobile toilets for a wedding have roomy, comfortable space that will not stain their evening wear, in which they can get themselves in order in maximum comfort and a pleasant atmosphere.

This comfort is gained by choosing luxury toilets for rent as we can supply them. These are luxurious, stylish mobile toilets that will contribute to elevating the event. In addition, these toilets are fitted with internal and external LED lighting and are air-conditioned, so they are pleasant to enter in every season of the year. Moreover, they include a ventilation system to remove bad odours, alongside an automatic air freshener that constantly spreads pleasant scents in the trailer. And if that is not enough, for the guests' enjoyment the toilet trailers are fitted with fine sound systems on which they can hear pleasant background music.

Beyond that, luxury toilet trailers come with an attendant who takes care of their functioning and cleanliness throughout the event.

Mobile Toilets for Weddings, Company Events and Celebrations

For your next event we can supply you with designed, modern mobile toilet trailers in various sizes. Choosing luxury mobile toilets can suit a variety of event types. For example, weddings in nature, outdoor bar and bat mitzvahs, concept events, conferences, team-building days, executive events, glittering parties, special shows and the like.

We place a strong emphasis on product quality and competitive market prices, with service and reliability guiding everything we do.
