Projects

Projects: mobile toilets at large events

Five large events where we set up a complete restroom operation: where, for how many people and what was supplied on site. From an event of thousands of guests to one with hundreds of thousands of participants.

Meron, Lag BaOmer

Location
Mount Meron compound and access routes
Attendance
About 500,000 worshippers and visitors

One of the largest and most complex mass events in Israel: an enormous crowd, mountainous terrain and a need for the highest standard of hygiene.

  1. Hundreds of mobile toilet units

    Transport and placement at key points, from the bus parking areas and the routes up the mountain to the bonfire compounds.

  2. Advance planning

    Working in full coordination with the security and rescue authorities, and deploying the equipment before the roads are closed and the crowds arrive.

  3. Round-the-clock maintenance

    Field teams for service, emptying, cleaning and maintenance throughout the days of the celebration.

Acre Festival

Location
The Old City, Acre
Attendance
About 25,000 visitors over the days of the festival

Setting up temporary infrastructure in an ancient city with narrow alleys and historic architecture. We have been supporting the festival for many years.

  1. Dozens of mobile toilet units

    Careful placement at key points across the Old City, with convenient access for visitors while keeping the crowd flowing between performance venues.

  2. Multi-day maintenance

    A reinforced field team for cleaning, emptying and ongoing maintenance during all hours of operation, from the start of the festival to its end.

Torch Lighting Ceremony

Location
Mount Herzl, Jerusalem
Attendance
About 4,000 guests, dignitaries and staff

A state ceremony that demands precise preparation. We operated in full coordination with the security and production teams, and in compliance with the required safety standards.

  1. Luxury VIP trailers

    For the production areas, hospitality areas and guests of honor.

  2. Mobile toilets

    Clean, accessible toilet units throughout Mount Herzl.

  3. Support from the setup stage

    Sanitary solutions for the crews who worked on site for days and weeks before the ceremony.

  4. Real-time maintenance

    Service, cleaning and maintenance throughout the setup days and during the ceremony itself.

Pride Parade, Tel Aviv

Location
The parade route and assembly areas across Tel Aviv
Attendance
About 170,000 celebrants and visitors

A street parade requires smart distribution across many points, operational flexibility and close cooperation with the municipality and security authorities.

  1. Hundreds of mobile toilet units

    Along the parade route, at the assembly areas and at the finish point, for quick access and shorter queues.

  2. VIP trailers for production and performers

    Luxury air-conditioned trailers backstage at the stage areas.

  3. Maintenance and cleaning

    Service staff and operations teams throughout all hours of operation.

Opera Festival, Yarkon Park

Location
Yarkon Park, Tel Aviv
Attendance
About 30,000 visitors and guests

A setup tailored to the size of the event: a combination of unit types enabled smart crowd routing and placement that does not harm the local nature.

  1. Chemical toilets

    Dozens of units at strategic points across the park, for a quick, accessible response for the general public.

  2. Luxury VIP trailers

    Backstage and in the hospitality areas, for the artists, the orchestra and guests of honor.

  3. Faucet stations

    Water stations for handwashing and freshening up throughout the festival.
