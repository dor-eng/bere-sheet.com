Hand-wash stations

Hand-Wash Station Rental for Events

Hand-wash stations for all types of events. Installation at the event site does not require a connection to water or sewage infrastructure, and use is simple and almost as convenient as at home.

How it works

The stations are installed together with a water tank used for hand washing, with no connection to water or sewage infrastructure.

Hand-wash station rental at Bereshit includes installation at the event site and an initial usage briefing. Rental is for a few weeks, a few days or a few hours.

Where should hand-wash stations be placed at the event site?

Hand-wash stations should stand where guests will want to wash their hands. Stations for washing hands after using the toilet should be placed next to the toilet units. A station near the food area lets guests wash their hands and freshen up before the meal.

The convenient location for the stations and toilet units is a level, lit and accessible area. In a natural setting with no lighting, the area of the toilet units and stations should be lit, for safe and comfortable use.

What will be comfortable for guests?

Chemical toilet units together with water hand-wash stations and mirrors provide the comfort of a well-equipped home restroom even out in nature. For the beach or another hospitality area you can rent VIP-design toilet units with stations that include a large enough water tank.

The number of stations and toilet units is set by the number of guests and the nature of the event. You can rent hand-wash stations together with chemical toilets or showers in a single package.
