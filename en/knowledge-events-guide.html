Mobile Toilets for Events - How Many Stalls You Need and Where to Place Them

Organising a successful event requires attention to many details, but one that is easy to forget is the mobile toilet solution, which ensures an aesthetic and sterile option for the guests.

Toilet Rental for Events – The Comprehensive Guide to Choosing Right

In this article we review everything you need to know about renting toilets for events, from the reasons to choose this solution to the rental process itself.

Portable Toilets and Mobile Toilets – What Are They?

Mobile toilets are temporary sanitary solutions, essential for events with no permanent infrastructure. They come in a range of models, from basic to deluxe, and suit festivals, weddings in nature, sporting events and more. Renting mobile toilets ensures convenience for attendees, compliance with regulations, and hygiene at small and large events alike.

Their advantages include flexibility in placement, suitability to the size of the event, and tailored solutions such as VIP stalls and accessible toilets. They allow organisers to focus on other aspects of the event, knowing that guests' sanitary needs are handled to a high standard. Choosing a professional supplier ensures comprehensive service, from the initial consultation to removal at the end of the event.

Why Rent Mobile Toilets for Events?

Renting portable toilets for events offers several significant advantages:

  • Convenience for attendees: provides a hygienic and convenient solution, especially at long events or events with many participants.
  • Regulatory compliance: ensures compliance with the requirements of the law and regulations regarding sanitary facilities at public events.
  • Flexibility: lets you match the number and quality of toilets to the needs of the specific event.
  • Protecting the environment: prevents pollution and damage to the surroundings, especially at events in nature or at historic sites.

Types of Portable Toilets

There is a wide range of options when it comes to mobile toilets for events:

  • Standard chemical toilets: the basic and most common solution, suitable for most events.
  • Deluxe mobile toilets (VIP): offer an upgraded experience with luxury fittings, suitable for elegant events.
  • Accessible toilets: designed specifically for people with disabilities, essential for meeting accessibility regulations.
  • Portable hand-washing stations: add another layer of hygiene, especially important at food events.

You can find all of the types mentioned above in Bereshit's range. Each type and model has its own advantages and disadvantages, so it is very important to understand the needs of the event and the guests and to choose the best match for the required model.

Considerations When Choosing Toilets for Events

When choosing mobile toilets for an event, several factors should be taken into account:

  • Number of participants and duration of the event: determine the number of units required.
  • Type and character of the event: affects the choice of toilet type (standard, VIP, etc.).
  • Location and site conditions: affect placement and access options.
  • Budget: dictates the level of service and the quantity possible.

It is important to weigh all of these factors to ensure an optimal solution that meets the needs of the event and its participants.

The Rental Process
