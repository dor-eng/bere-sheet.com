Mobile Toilets: Bereshit

Bereshit is pleased to present the new generation of mobile toilets in Israel.

Our leading motto is to give you, the customer, solutions for every event, professional service, excellence and a personal touch all the way. Bereshit rents toilet facilities for both the short term and the long term, for any purpose, finding solutions that meet your requirements.

Our team will take care of cleaning, maintenance and operation of the toilet facilities and leave them hygienic, shiny and pleasantly scented. Our toilets are suited to events such as: festivals, camping in nature, summer day camps, ceremonies, bicycle races, mass events and more...

Who does not know the urgent need to relieve oneself? This need can arise at a concert by a beloved, famous band in some deserted orchard, at an outdoor event with a feast and lavish refreshments, during an organised trip with a class or a large group of people sleeping in the open, or even during work in field conditions, such as construction and development work. Suddenly we feel unwell or a pressing feeling in the stomach, and look for a place where we can relieve ourselves. If no such building exists in the area, we find ourselves in a problem.

Why Do You Need Mobile Toilets?

Mobile toilets answer a basic human need that does not take the changing environment into account. So, for example, whether we are at a concert in the field or at a building job, at some point we will need the privacy and comfort of mobile toilets, the quiet that is so needed at that moment. This is a necessary and important facility that can make any event calmer and more comfortable.

How Do We Choose Mobile Toilets?

Before placing an order for mobile toilets, there are several parameters that are important to take into account. First, you need to understand what purpose the mobile toilets are being ordered for. It is important to find out how many people the toilets are meant to serve (for example, for a large event we would order more than one mobile toilet unit), what exactly the field conditions are, what the event times are (morning versus evening, light versus dark) and so on. It is also important to take special populations into account, such as people with disabilities and the elderly, and make sure we can make the toilets accessible for them too.

Types of Mobile Toilets

There is a wide range of mobile toilets that can suit any event. Each mobile toilet rental order has a different price matching the trailer you ordered and the length of the order. As noted, there are many types of mobile toilets: they can be regular toilets, intended for building or field workers. We can also order toilets with a ramp, making them accessible to people with disabilities or older people. There are also more luxurious mobile toilets, suited to events such as weddings in the field.

Mobile Toilets: Price List

As we already mentioned, when renting mobile toilets, prices may range widely, reflecting a combination of many factors such as: the level of the trailer, the type of trailer, the length of the order, and so on.
