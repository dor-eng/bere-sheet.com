Accessible toilets

Accessible mobile toilets

An accessible toilet stall is not a luxury but a necessity. At a mass event, a construction site or a temporary public venue, you need to provide accessible toilets that are available and convenient, for comfortable and safe use by people with disabilities.

Maneuvering space, grab bars and adapted fittings

An accessible toilet stall is designed to meet the needs of people with physical disabilities. Its main features: more maneuvering space, supportive grab bars and adapted fittings.

In Israel, accessibility regulations require installing accessible toilet stalls in public buildings and at mass events.

How to make a mobile toilet stall accessible

  1. A larger stall

    Large enough for a wheelchair to move freely: at least 150×150 cm, for a full turn.

  2. Ramp

    A sturdy ramp allows easy access into the stall.

  3. Grab bars

    Sturdy bars for support and safety.

  4. Raised toilet seat

    To ease the transfer from the wheelchair.

  5. Wide door

    For comfortable wheelchair entry.

  6. Fittings at a suitable height

    Sink, mirror and toilet paper dispenser.

  7. Safety

    An emergency button and adequate lighting.

A single accessible stall or a luxury trailer with a lift

Our range includes a chemical toilet unit in an accessible model. For larger events there is a 4-stall accessible luxury restroom trailer, with a dedicated entrance for people with disabilities and an advanced lift, for events of up to 350 guests.

In the 4-stall trailer the accessible stall is equipped with an electric lift, is wider than usual and includes grab rails and adapted maneuvering space.

Luxury restroom trailers

  1. Accessibility for everyone

    People with disabilities take full part in events and public activities.

  2. Regulatory compliance

    Compliance with the requirements of the law and the regulations on accessibility.

  3. Convenient location

    Place the stalls in a spot that is easy to reach and lit in the evening hours.

Frequently asked questions about accessible toilets

Are luxury toilets accessible to people with disabilities?

Yes. There is a unique model equipped with an advanced lift, which allows comfortable entry for people with disabilities too.

What is the minimum size of an accessible stall?

The stall must be large enough, at least 150×150 cm, to allow a wheelchair to turn fully.

Are accessible stalls also suitable for construction sites?

Yes. At construction sites the model is chosen according to the specification, and accessible stalls are arranged in line with the safety and accessibility regulations.
