Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

We want everyone to be able to use our site and service with ease. This page details the adjustments that were made, what has not yet been checked, and how to contact us about accessibility.

This is a translation; the Hebrew version is the binding text.

What was adapted on the site

  • The site was built according to Israeli Standard 5568 (based on the WCAG 2.1 guidelines) at level AA. On 24.10.2026 all 16 pages of the site were checked, in phone view and computer view, with an automated testing tool according to the WCAG 2.1 rules at levels A and AA: no serious defects were found.
  • A "Skip to content" link at the top of every page, and an orderly heading structure with a single main heading on each page.
  • Full keyboard navigation, including the menu, with a clear indication of the current location.
  • Alternative text for images and icons.
  • Buttons and links at least 24 pixels in size on a phone.
  • Anyone who has set "reduce motion" on their device will receive the site without scroll animations.
  • The Hebrew version of the site is written right to left, and the site is adapted for phone, tablet and computer.

Accessibility arrangements in the service

We provide accessible toilet units for events and construction sites, as well as a luxury trailer with an accessible unit and a lift.

What has not yet been checked

  • The site was checked with an automated testing tool and a manual pass in the Chrome, Safari and Samsung Internet browsers, including a real phone.
  • Keyboard-only navigation was checked (skip to content, menu, the quote form and the quantity calculator), as well as the page structure for assistive technologies: link names, labels for every field and messages that update in real time.
  • Not yet tested in practice with a screen reader or magnification software.
