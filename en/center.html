Mobile toilet rental in the Center

Planning an event in central Israel at a venue with no decent toilet facilities, or no water and sewage connection at all? Luxury trailers and units that blend into the venue and preserve a sense of elegance.

From business events to weddings

Luxury restrooms in the Center suit business events: concept events, executive events, team-building days, performances, parties, launches, fairs and conferences. The emphasis is on outdoor events, in nature or at venues not equipped for a large crowd.

They are also suitable for relatively large family events, such as a bar or bat mitzvah or a wedding.

The Tamar luxury unit

An exceptionally large toilet unit that greatly improves the experience of using it. A recirculating flush system rinses the toilet after use, with a cover at the bottom of the bowl that conceals its contents.

  1. Handwashing sink

    A sink can be added.

  2. Shelf and mirror

    A shelf for personal items and a fixed mirror on the door.

  3. Ventilation

    Ventilation grilles all around.

  4. Toilet paper

    A large dispenser for three rolls.

Fittings and service package

Luxury units with fragrance dispensers, interior and exterior LED lighting and a sound system. The service package includes a maintenance attendant responsible for cleanliness during the event.

We are also happy to advise on the number of units required for your guest count, on arranging the units on site and on costs.
