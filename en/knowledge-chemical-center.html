Portable Chemical Toilets for Rent in Central Israel and Nationwide

Organising and producing an event in field conditions is a long and complicated process, a demanding task even for the most experienced and veteran event producers.

Portable toilets for events

In the field, none of the resources our usual environment offers are available. We therefore need to find creative production solutions in a range of areas: electricity, water, accommodation, food and more. Among all these things, one that tends to be forgotten is a place to relieve oneself. Because proper toilets are so taken for granted in our urban lives, when we produce an event in the field we need to find a suitable, orderly solution that lets participants use a proper toilet stall. That is exactly what portable toilet rental services for events are for.

What exactly are portable toilets?

Portable field toilets are toilet stalls that are not connected to a central sewage system. As their name suggests, portable toilets can be transported on a truck or other delivery vehicle, and can be placed in a variety of locations and field conditions. All these characteristics make portable chemical toilets a perfect solution for events held in nature or in field conditions. Waste is removed from portable toilets using a special pumping unit.

Which events are we talking about?

Portable toilets are a common, readily available solution for a wide range of events. An event in nature is an exceptional experience that takes us out of the routine and becomes a wonderful memory. For this reason, many events choose locations with a natural feel and field conditions, and there are many examples: many couples choose to hold the happiest event of their lives, their wedding day, in nature, to create a memorable event in a beautiful landscape. In addition to weddings, many events such as bar mitzvahs, VIP launches, company team-building and fun days for employees, large family gatherings and many more take place every day at the many nature sites across the country. Beyond these events, portable toilets for rent are also in demand for camping. When a large group of people goes to a camp in nature, as part of a youth movement trip or a school trip within the education system, portable camping toilets are used there as well. In recent years the country has also seen significant momentum in the festival field. A festival is an event where many people gather to listen to music or take part in a shared activity, and festivals usually take place at various sites in nature. Organising a large festival is not complete without an adequate portable toilet solution.

Portable toilets: not only for events

In addition to one-off events held in nature, portable toilets also serve work sites located where there is no access to regular plumbing and sewage. Various construction sites, where many workers labour for long days, must have somewhere to relieve themselves. Most of these sites have no plumbing system because it has not yet been built. Another example of such a site is an archaeological dig, where many people stay for an extended period and of course need a place to relieve themselves.

Different types of portable toilets

Because portable toilets are used at many different kinds of events and camps, there are different models that address the needs of each event more precisely:

  • Luxury toilet trailer: at events where a prestigious atmosphere matters, such as a wedding or a VIP launch, there is no need to compromise on luxury even when it comes to the toilet stall. A luxury toilet trailer contains two toilet stalls, inviting in design, creating a well-appointed atmosphere. The trailer is connected to air conditioning and running water, which allow comfortable use of the toilets inside. To install a luxury portable toilet trailer, a connection to electricity and a water hose is required, of the kind available at many field sites.
  • Portable chemical toilets: the standard portable toilet model uses various chemicals that break down human waste and mask unpleasant odours. This type of toilet, which Bereshit offers its customers, is in fact an ecological portable toilet. Ecological toilets take environmental protection into account and use substances that are harmless and non-polluting. The chemicals used meet the strictest regulatory requirements, which ensure that their use does not harm the environment and of course poses no danger to the users of the toilets.

Portable toilets: a business for professionals

Working with portable toilets for various events requires expertise and experience. Because these toilets deal with materials and waste, there is no room for mistakes or for unprofessional, amateur installation of the stalls. Any amateur mistake can lead to clearly unpleasant results that overshadow an entire event. It is therefore necessary to use professionals who can install portable toilet stalls in any field conditions, follow all the guidelines and rules involved in their use, and manage waste pumping smoothly and professionally, using modern and advanced equipment.
