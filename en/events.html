Events

Mobile toilet rental for events

One of the details that is easy to forget when organizing an event is the toilets. Mobile toilets are a temporary sanitary solution for events without permanent infrastructure, in a range of models from basic to luxury, ensuring comfort for guests and good hygiene at events small and large.

From a wedding in nature to a festival

Mobile toilet rental is essential at weddings and bar mitzvahs, business and corporate events, parties, sports events and festivals. Luxury toilets are especially suited to premium events such as weddings in nature, music festivals and VIP areas.

Mobile toilets make it possible to hold events in unique outdoor locations, without building permanent plumbing and sewage infrastructure. At events in nature and at historic sites they also prevent pollution and damage to the environment.

What we place at an event

  1. Standard chemical toilets

    The basic and most common solution, suitable for most events.

  2. Luxury toilets (VIP)

    An upgraded experience with premium fittings, for elegant events. Luxury restroom trailers

  3. Accessible toilets

    Designed especially for people with disabilities, and essential for meeting accessibility regulations. Accessible mobile toilets

  4. Mobile handwashing stations

    Add another layer of hygiene, and are especially important at food events.

Matching the number of guests is the most important measure

As a rule of thumb, for events of up to 200 guests we recommend placing 2-3 toilet stalls. For an event of about 500 guests, increase the quantity to at least 5 stalls, and continue at a similar ratio as the number of people grows.

The accepted formula is 1-2 stalls per 100 guests at an event lasting several hours. Plan generously: it is always better to order an extra unit than to regret long queues.

The number of guests and the duration of the event determine the number of units; the type of event affects the type of toilets; the location and ground conditions affect placement and access options.

Clean and in working order throughout the event

Toilet rental does not end with placing the stall. We offer operating services that include a dedicated staff member responsible for ongoing cleaning and for checking the stalls in real time, hermetic pumping of the holding tank and renewal of consumables. You can also upgrade with automatic air fresheners and background music.

At a small event of a few hours, ongoing maintenance is usually not needed. At a large, long or especially upscale event, make sure the service includes ongoing maintenance and cleaning. Emptying the holding tank is especially important at events that last many days.

Frequently asked questions about event toilets

How do chemical toilets work without a sewage connection?

The toilets operate as a self-contained system that includes a sealed holding tank with a dedicated chemical solution. The solution breaks down organic waste, disinfects the tank and neutralizes odors, and upper vents allow air circulation.

What are the parameters for choosing the number and type of stalls?

The number of guests, at a ratio of 1-2 stalls per 100 people; the nature of the activity, luxury trailers for festive events; and the need for accessibility for people with disabilities or an older population.

Will you advise us on matching the number of stalls?

Yes. We recommend using our professional team to match the number of stalls to the number of guests at the event.

Is the maintenance service included in the price?

Not always. Before ordering, make sure the price includes cleaning and disinfection, a thorough check before delivery, and pumping at the frequency you want. What goes into the price

Are there accessible toilets for people with disabilities in the luxury equipment too?

Yes. The 4-stall trailer has a dedicated accessible stall with an electric lift, wider than usual, with grab rails and adapted maneuvering space.

How does renting mobile toilets save costs?

It makes it possible to hold events in unique outdoor locations without the rental fees of event gardens, and removes the need to build permanent plumbing and sewage infrastructure.
