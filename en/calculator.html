How many stalls do you need?

Enter how many guests will attend your event or site and get an initial estimate of the number of toilet stalls and the suitable trailer model. The recommendation is based on the rules Bereshit publishes, and the final fit is settled with the team.

The recommendation updates instantly; there is nothing to submit.

The calculator requires JavaScript. The full rules appear below.

What the recommendation is based on

  1. Up to 200 guests: 2-3 stalls

    As a rule of thumb, for events or sites of up to 200 guests we recommend placing 2-3 toilet stalls. For an event of about 500 guests, increase to at least 5 stalls, and continue at a similar ratio.

  2. 1-2 stalls per 100 guests

    The accepted formula is 1-2 stalls per 100 guests at an event lasting several hours. Plan generously: an extra unit is better than long queues.

  3. Luxury restroom trailers by guest count

    A 2-stall trailer for up to 200 guests, a 4-stall accessible trailer with a lift for up to 350 guests, a 6-stall trailer for up to 400 guests.

For construction sites, the site does not give a fixed ratio per number of workers. The team will match the quantity to the number of workers on site. Services for construction sites
