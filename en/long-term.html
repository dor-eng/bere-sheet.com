Long-term rental

Long-term mobile toilet rental

When you rent toilet stalls from us for the long term, we provide a professional service of pumping and ongoing maintenance, to keep the units usable, comfortable and hygienic.

For projects that last over time

You can rent the units short-term for one-off jobs, or long-term for active construction sites, according to the schedule in the field. A 4-stall accessible luxury restroom trailer can also be rented for long periods, and includes pumping and maintenance.

Mobile toilets for a construction site · Luxury restroom trailers

Usually a more favorable rate

The rental period affects pricing. Usually, long-term rental is at a more favorable rate. In a long rental it is important to keep up ongoing maintenance and pumping of the waste tank, and at times this service carries an additional cost, so it is worth confirming in advance what is included in the price.

The site has no fixed monthly price list. The price is set in a quote according to the type of stalls, the quantity, the rental duration and the location.

What goes into the price of mobile toilets

What the ongoing service includes

A maintenance package that includes pumping, cleaning, disinfecting and renewing chemicals on an ongoing basis, to keep a high level of hygiene throughout the rental period. The collection tank is emptied periodically using dedicated pumps.
