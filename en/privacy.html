What information we collect

When you fill in the contact form, we receive your name, phone number and the content of your message. Providing the details is voluntary and not a legal obligation, but without contact details we will not be able to get back to you.

There is no need to include sensitive information in the message. The details of the event or project are enough.

Cookies and measurement tools

On your first visit to the site we will ask whether you agree to measurement and advertising tools, for example Google Analytics or the Meta pixel. Such tools use cookies that help us understand how the site is used and show relevant ads. They will be activated only after you agree. If you decline, the site will work exactly the same.

Your choice is stored only in your own browser for 180 days. You can change it at any time through the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page.

In this version of the site, no measurement or advertising tool is operating yet. Before we activate such a tool, we will state here its name, its purpose and how long the information is kept.

You are entitled to review the information held about you and to ask to correct or delete it, under the Privacy Protection Law, 1981. Contact us by phone or at the email address above.
